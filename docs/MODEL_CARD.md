# Model Card: InvoiceGuard Multimodal Risk Fusion Engine

## Model Details
- **Model Name:** InvoiceGuard Multimodal Risk Fusion Engine (`fusion_xgb`)
- **Version:** 0.2.0 (Day 2 Milestone Release)
- **Model Architecture:** Hybrid Dual-Track Ensemble
  1. **Deterministic Baseline:** Bounded probabilistic noisy-OR signal aggregation.
  2. **Machine Learning Classifier:** Gradient-boosted decision trees (`XGBClassifier`) with strict **monotone increasing constraints** on all 31 features, calibrated via 5-fold **Isotonic Regression** (`CalibratedClassifierCV`).
  3. **Escalation Rules:** Hard floors for critical findings (e.g. `BANK_ACCOUNT_SHARED_WITH_OTHER_VENDOR`, `MODIFIED_DUPLICATE`) and multiple concurrent high-risk signals.
- **Explainability:** TreeExplainer SHAP values computing top feature contributions, paired with rule-level evidence payloads and plain-English narratives.
- **License:** MIT License.

---

## Intended Use
- **Primary Use Case:** Automated risk scoring and evidence localization on Accounts Payable (AP) invoices to assist human compliance and finance reviewers.
- **Decision Support Contract:** InvoiceGuard reports **risk indicators**, never definitive fraud verdicts.
  > *"InvoiceGuard flags anomalies for human review. It does not determine fraud."*
- **Target Users:** Internal audit teams, finance controllers, procurement operations, AP automation systems.

---

## Input Signals & Features
The model operates on a **31-dimensional feature vector** synthesized from 7 specialized backend detection engines:

### 1. Engine Signals (Scores & Confidences)
- `financial_score`, `financial_conf`: Mathematical arithmetic, subtotal/grand total summation, amount-in-words translation.
- `tax_identity_score`, `tax_identity_conf`: GSTIN structure & Mod-36 checksum, PAN matching, date-aware GST slabs, interstate/intrastate tax rules.
- `identifiers_score`, `identifiers_conf`: Invoice number reuse, sequence counter regressions, post-dating, due date conflicts.
- `duplicate_score`, `duplicate_conf`: 4-level deduplication (SHA-256 hash, perceptual image hash, fuzzy token matching, semantic vector cosine similarity).
- `vendor_score`, `vendor_conf`: Historical transaction volume, median amount deviation (MAD z-score), Isolation Forest anomaly scores, lookalike vendor detection.
- `bank_change_score`, `bank_change_conf`: Unseen bank accounts, account switching cadence, cross-vendor shared bank accounts.
- `visual_score`, `visual_conf`: PDF producer manipulation traces, Error-Level Analysis (ELA), font incongruities, coverage patches.

### 2. Engineered Meta-Features
- `finding_count`, `critical_count`, `high_count`, `medium_count`, `severity_mass`.
- `signals_above_50`, `signals_above_70`, `max_signal_score`, `min_signal_conf`, `score_variance`.
- Critical flag indicators: `flag_shared_bank`, `flag_exact_dup`, `flag_lookalike_vendor`, `flag_gstin_invalid`, `flag_amount_outlier`.
- `round_total_flag`, `item_count`, `extraction_confidence`.

### 3. Monotone Constraint Guarantee
All 31 features are constrained to be monotonically non-decreasing (`monotone_constraints = (1, 1, ..., 1)`). This mathematically guarantees that increasing anomaly evidence or risk signals **can never lower** the predicted risk score.

## Training Data & Methodology
- **Dataset:** 800 synthetic invoices generated by the InvoiceGuard deterministic simulation engine (`ml/synthetic/`):
  - 300 genuine invoices reflecting 45 simulated Indian business profiles across 5 distinct layout templates.
  - 300 tampered invoices injected with 16 distinct anomaly operators across re-render, pixel-edit, and PDF-structure modes.
  - 200 visual comparison pairs.
- **Partitioning:** Grouped split by vendor ID and template ID into Train (592), Test (112), and Validation (96) to prevent data leakage.
- **Artifact Size:** < 0.1 MB (`ml/artifacts/fusion_xgb.joblib`), comfortably below the 10 MB repository threshold.

---

## Evaluation & Performance Highlights
Evaluated against the held-out test split (112 unseen documents across unseen vendors/templates):
- **PR-AUC:** 0.6612
- **Precision @ Threshold 30 (Medium Risk):** 0.625
- **Recall @ Threshold 30:** 1.000 (100% detection of tampered test documents)
- **Per-Fraud-Type Recall:** 1.000 across all 16 distinct tamper operators.
- **Inference Latency:** p50 = 26 ms, p95 = 40 ms per invoice.
- Complete metric breakdown and ablation study documented in `docs/EVALUATION.md`.

## Limitations & Ethical Considerations
1. **Synthetic Data Bias:** The model is trained and calibrated on synthetic invoice generators. Real-world commercial variations, handwriting, and physical scanning noise may exhibit distribution shift.
2. **Cold-Start Vendors:** Vendors with fewer than 5 historical transactions receive population-level statistical baselines rather than individualized profiling.
3. **No Automatic Rejections:** High risk scores must trigger human review workflows rather than automated payment cancellations.
4. **Data Privacy:** Banking account numbers are hashed; only masked representations (`XXXXXX1234`) are exposed in outputs.
